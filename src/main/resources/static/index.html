THE EDUPATH VISION

Preserving Experience, Empowering Growth

In university education, each incoming batch often repeats the same uncertainties: when to begin DSA preparation, which core subjects define placement screenings, and how to balance academic CGPA with competitive coding and project portfolios.

EduPath creates an institutional memory. Senior students and placed alumni document their journeys into structured blueprints, while students receive personalized mentorship and verified guidance.

Role-based authentication in the database ensures that every student and mentor interacts through a role-tailored interface designed specifically for their needs.

🗺️ Semester Roadmaps Progressive milestones from Semester 1 fundamentals to final placement readiness.
📖 Guidance Playbooks Detailed accounts of interview rounds, preparation tips, and verified advice.
💬 Verified Q&A Ask questions across Academics, Internships, Higher Studies, and Placements.
🛡️ Role-Based Access Secure authentication automatically determines Junior vs Senior workflows.

⚡ Running on Spring Boot & 8-Table Schema

Spring Data JPA, Hibernate ORM, and Spring Security with BCrypt & JWT.
